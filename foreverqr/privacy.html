The short version

QR codes are created and scanned entirely in your browser. The text, links, Wi-Fi passwords, contact details, logos and images you use never leave your device. We don’t have accounts, so we don’t hold any profile about you.

What we do not collect

  • The content of any QR code you generate or scan.
  • Uploaded logos or images (processed locally, never uploaded).
  • Camera footage (the camera feed is decoded on your device and never recorded or transmitted).

What may be collected

  • Waitlist email (optional). If you join the dynamic-codes waitlist, your email address is sent to our form provider [PROVIDER NAME] solely to tell you when the product launches. You can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Privacy-friendly analytics (if enabled). We may use [GoatCounter / Plausible], which counts page views and simple events (for example “a code was downloaded”) without cookies and without collecting personal data or the contents of your codes.
  • Hosting logs. Our host [GitHub Pages / other] may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security. See their privacy policy.

Cookies and local storage

We don’t use cookies. Your browser stores only your light/dark theme choice locally on your device.

Your rights
