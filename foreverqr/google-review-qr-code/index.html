Make a Google review QR code (with the official link)

Most customers are happy to leave a review if it takes ten seconds. A QR code at the counter that opens Google’s review box directly gets you there.

Opens the generator set to a website link with the caption “Leave us a review on Google”. Paste the link from step 1 below.

Make a review QR code

Step 1: Get your review link from Google

Google gives each Business Profile its own review link. According to Google’s Business Profile Help, on a computer:

  1. Go to your Business Profile at business.google.com.
  2. Select Read reviews, then Get more reviews.
  3. Select the copy icon to copy the review link.

The same panel offers a QR code to download, but Google notes it can only be generated in a computer browser, not on mobile. If Google’s code is all you need, use it. Make your own when you want a print-ready sign with a caption, a vector SVG for a print shop, or your brand colours.

Use the link Google gives you, not the address of your Maps listing. The review link opens the “write a review” box directly; a Maps link makes customers hunt for the button.

Step 2: Turn it into a QR code

  1. Open the generator with the button above and paste the review link.
  2. Check the line under the preview: it should show the Google link you copied.
  3. Scan the preview with your phone. It should open your business’s review form.
  4. Download the printable sign (6 × 8 inches, with the caption and the destination underneath) or a PNG/SVG for your own design.

Where to put it

Google suggests sharing the link or code on receipts, in thank-you emails, at the end of chats, and printed in your store. In practice, the best spots are where a happy customer has a free moment:

  • The counter or card reader, at the moment of paying.
  • Takeaway bags, boxes and receipts.
  • Tables, waiting areas and fitting rooms.
  • Invoices and follow-up emails for service businesses (use the link itself; a code in an email is useless to someone reading it on their phone).

Rules to know

  • No incentives. Google’s help page says offering free or discounted goods or services in exchange for reviews is prohibited, as is changing or removing reviews in exchange for incentives. Don’t print “10% off for a 5-star review”.
  • Customers need a Google Account to leave a review, so some people will need to sign in first.
  • Ask everyone. A plain “Tell us how we did” sign that every customer sees is both fairer and safer than only asking people who look happy.

Print size

On a counter sign read at arm’s length, 4–5 cm (about 2 in) is comfortable. A common rule of thumb is a scanning distance of about 10 times the code’s width, so a wall sign read from 1.5 metres needs about 15 cm.

FAQ

Where do I find my Google review link?

In your Google Business Profile on a computer: Read reviews, then Get more reviews, then the copy icon. That link opens the review box directly.

Doesn’t Google already give me a QR code?

Yes, from a computer browser. It is fine to use. Making your own lets you add a caption, download a print-ready sign or SVG, and match your colours.

Can I offer a discount for reviews?

No. Google’s Business Profile Help says offering free or discounted goods or services in exchange for reviews is prohibited.

Will the review QR code expire?

The code made here is static and can’t expire. It keeps working as long as Google keeps your review link active for your profile.
