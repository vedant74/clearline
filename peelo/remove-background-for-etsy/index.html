Remove the background from Etsy listing photos

Etsy doesn't require a white background, and handmade products often sell better in context. A clean cut-out is still the quickest way to a tidy shop grid and a first photo that stands out in search. Here's when to use one and how.

White, off-white or lifestyle?

Unlike Amazon, Etsy lets you use any background, so choose based on the product and on what shoppers see in search.

  • Clean background for small items that get lost in busy scenes (jewellery, pins, stickers, ceramics) and for shops that want a uniform grid.
  • Soft off-white or a muted brand colour if pure white looks clinical next to your handmade style. Peelo's warm grey swatch (#F2EFE9) works well for this.
  • Lifestyle photo for later images in the listing, showing scale, texture and use. A clean first photo plus lifestyle shots after it is a common winning combination.

Size and shape that work on Etsy

Etsy's guidance for listing photos is to use images at least 2000 pixels on the shortest side, in a landscape 4:3 or a square 1:1 ratio. Search results and your shop page show a cropped thumbnail, and you can adjust that crop in the listing editor. Keep the product centred with some margin so the crop doesn't cut it off. A square with the product filling about 80% is a safe starting point.

Peelo keeps your original resolution, so start from a photo where the product is large. A modern phone photo with the product filling most of the frame easily gives a square over 2000 px.

Keep your shop grid consistent

Shoppers judge a shop at a glance. Listing photos with matching backgrounds and the same product size look professional even when the photos came from different days and lighting. To get there:

  • Use the same background colour for every first photo. Note the hex code of your custom colour.
  • Use the same Subject fills percentage per product type, for example 80% for mugs and 70% for prints so the frame shows.
  • Shoot from the same angle, usually straight on or a three-quarter view from slightly above.

Handmade products that need extra care

  • Fuzzy or fibre items (knits, macramé, plush): the AI may soften the edges. Raise Edge softness a little for a natural look rather than a hard, cut-paper edge.
  • Jewellery with fine chains: thin chains can drop out. Use Restore with a small brush, or keep a lifestyle background for that photo.
  • Glass and resin: transparent parts can disappear or keep bits of the old background. Check closely, or shoot on a plain contrasting backdrop.
  • Prints and flat art: you usually want a mock-up rather than a cut-out. Cut-outs are best for physical objects.

How to do it with Peelo

  1. Open the tool with the Etsy starting preset (white, square, 80% fill).
  2. Add your photo. It's processed in your browser and never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  3. Pick your shop background. Keep white, choose the warm grey swatch, or set your brand colour with Custom.
  4. Fix any edges in Refine edges: Erase removes leftover background, and Restore brings back parts of the product.
  5. Download JPG and upload it as the first listing photo. Then use Etsy's thumbnail adjust to centre the crop.

Questions

Does Etsy require a white background?

No. Etsy allows any background. White or a consistent plain colour helps small items stand out in search and keeps your shop grid tidy, but lifestyle photos are fine and often work well as later images.

What size should Etsy photos be?

Etsy recommends images at least 2000 pixels on the shortest side, in a 4:3 or square ratio. Peelo keeps your original resolution, so start with a photo where the product is large in the frame.

Can I process a whole shop's photos at once?

Yes. Batch mode takes many photos with one set of settings and gives you a single ZIP. It's free for 3 photos at a time; Batch Pro ($19 once, no subscription) handles up to 200 per run.

Will Etsy know I edited the background?

Editing a background is normal product photography. Just make sure the photo still shows the item accurately: colour, size and condition.
