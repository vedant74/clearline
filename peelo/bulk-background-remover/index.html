Bulk background remover: a whole folder of photos at once

Doing product photos one by one gets old fast. Peelo's batch mode takes a set of photos, applies the same settings to every one, and gives you a single ZIP with your original filenames. It runs in your browser, so nothing is uploaded.

What batch mode does

  • One set of settings for every photo: transparent, white, any colour or a blurred background; PNG or JPG; an optional maximum size.
  • Marketplace squares: tick one box to get white, square, centred product shots, which is what Amazon, Etsy and Shopify listings usually want.
  • One ZIP at the end, with each file named after your original plus a suffix, so it's easy to match results to products.
  • Automatic clean-up: leftover background text and specks are removed for you, which matters when shelves, signs or price tags are in the shot.
  • No upload: every photo is processed on your device. That's useful for unreleased products, client work or anything under NDA.

How long does a batch take?

Each photo is processed on your own computer, so speed depends on your device. With a graphics chip the browser can use (WebGPU), photos typically take a few seconds each; on processor-only devices, expect longer per photo. A batch of 100 can take anywhere from a few minutes to about 20 on slower machines. Keep the tab open while it runs. You can pause and resume.

Getting consistent results across a catalogue

  • Shoot on a plain background with even light. Any remover does better when the subject clearly stands out.
  • Keep the product a similar size in frame across shots. With Marketplace square on, Peelo centres each product at the same fill.
  • Spot-check a few results before uploading the lot. For the odd hard photo (glass, fine hair), open it in the single-photo tool and touch it up with the Erase and Restore brush.

How to remove backgrounds in bulk

  1. Open the Batch tab in Peelo.
  2. Drop in your photos, or choose a folder's worth with the file picker.
  3. Choose settings once: background (transparent, white or colour), Marketplace square on or off, PNG or JPG, and maximum size.
  4. Run the batch. The first run downloads the AI model once (about 91 MB). Keep the tab open; you'll see progress for each photo.
  5. Download the ZIP with all results, named after your original files.

Questions

How many photos can I process at once?

3 per run for free, or up to 200 per run with Batch Pro.

Is Batch Pro a subscription?

No. It's a one-time $19 payment, processed by Stripe. It unlocks in the browser you buy it in, and there's a 14-day refund if it doesn't work for you.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Peelo runs the AI model in your browser, so photos stay on your device during the whole batch.

Do results keep full resolution?

Yes, up to 16 megapixels per photo, unless you set a smaller maximum size.

Can I use it for Amazon, Etsy or Shopify listings?

Yes. Use a white background with Marketplace square on for centred, square product photos, then check your marketplace's current image rules before uploading.
