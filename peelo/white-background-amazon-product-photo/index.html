Make a pure white background for your Amazon main image

Amazon is strict about the first image on a listing. It needs a pure white background, and the product should fill most of the frame. Here's how to get there from an ordinary phone photo, free and without uploading anything.

What Amazon asks for in the main image

Amazon's product image requirements for the main image (the one shown in search results) boil down to a few rules:

  • Pure white background, RGB 255, 255, 255. Off-white, light grey or a slightly warm white from your lightbox doesn't count.
  • The product fills about 85% of the frame. Big margins make your thumbnail look smaller than competitors'.
  • At least 1000 pixels on the longest side is recommended so shoppers can zoom. Smaller images are accepted down to a lower minimum, but they lose zoom.
  • JPEG is preferred. PNG, TIFF and non-animated GIF are also accepted.
  • Only the product. No added text, logos, watermarks or props that aren't included in the sale.

Secondary images (lifestyle shots, infographics) have looser rules. This guide covers the main image, which is where most suppressed listings come from.

Why "almost white" gets flagged

A photo of a product on white card almost never has a white background in the file. The camera reads it as something like RGB 240, 238, 235, with a gradient toward the corners. Turning up brightness pushes the product's own highlights to white too. The reliable fix is to cut the product out and put it on a fresh canvas filled with exactly 255, 255, 255. Peelo's Marketplace photo mode does exactly that, and the JPG it exports has a true white background.

Getting the 85% fill right

"Fill" means the product's longest side takes up about 85% of the image's width or height. With Peelo's square framing, the tool finds the edges of the cut-out product and builds a square around it, so the longest side is exactly the percentage you pick. A tall bottle touches 85% of the height; a wide tray touches 85% of the width. You can change the fill with the Subject fills slider.

Resolution: plan for zoom

Peelo never upscales. The square is built from your original pixels, so the output size depends on how big the product is in your photo. If the product spans 900 px in the original, an 85% square comes out at about 1060 px, which is just enough for zoom. Fill the frame when you shoot, or use a higher-resolution camera mode. The tool shows the output size before you download.

Shooting tips that make the cut-out cleaner

  • Shoot against a plain background that contrasts with the product. A white product on white card is the hardest case for any AI.
  • Use soft, even light from two sides to cut down hard shadows. The cut-out removes the background, but shadows sitting on the product stay.
  • Avoid see-through packaging and glass where you can. Small on-device models find transparency hard, so check these edges carefully.
  • Keep the camera level with the product's centre to reduce distortion.

How to do it with Peelo

  1. Open the tool in Marketplace photo mode. The preset sets a white background, a square canvas and 85% fill.
  2. Add your product photo by dropping it in, choosing it, or pasting it. It's processed on your device.
  3. Check the edges. Drag the before/after slider. If a strap, handle or label edge is missing, use Refine edges, then Restore. Paint over leftover background with Erase.
  4. Adjust the fill if needed. 85% suits most products. Very thin items, like pens, may look better at 80% so the ends aren't cramped.
  5. Download JPG. Check the size shown under the buttons. Aim for at least 1000 px for zoom.

Questions

Is Peelo's white background exactly RGB 255, 255, 255?

Yes. In Marketplace photo mode the canvas is filled with #FFFFFF before the product is placed, and the JPG export keeps that pure white background. The soft edge pixels around the product blend from the product into white, as they would in a studio shot.

Should I upload PNG or JPG to Amazon?

Amazon prefers JPEG for product images. Use the JPG download for the main image. Keep a transparent PNG only if you'll be compositing the product elsewhere.

Why is my output smaller than 1000 pixels?

Peelo builds the square from your original pixels and doesn't upscale. If the product is small in the photo, the square will be small. Shoot closer or at a higher resolution so the product spans at least 850 px in the original.

Can I add a drop shadow?

Not yet. A shadow isn't required for the main image, and many top listings use a clean cut-out on white.
