remove.bg is shutting down on 1 December 2026: what to do now

remove.bg's own pricing page says the standalone website will no longer be available from 1 December 2026 at 9:00am CET, and that background removal is moving to Canva. Here's a short checklist so nothing gets lost, and what you can use afterwards.

What we actually know

The confirmed facts are short. remove.bg's pricing page says the standalone remove.bg website will no longer be available from 1 December 2026 at 9:00am CET, and that background removal is moving into Canva. That's it. We haven't seen official details here about every plan, unused credits or API customers, so treat anything beyond that as unconfirmed and check remove.bg's own notice and support pages for your account.

Plenty of articles go further and predict what Canva will charge or which features disappear. Some of that may turn out right, but it isn't confirmed, so we've left it out.

Before 1 December: a five-minute checklist

  • Download anything you still need. Past results, edited images and especially invoices or receipts for your accounting. Once the website is gone you may not be able to get them.
  • Check your credits and plan. If you have unused credits or a subscription, read remove.bg's notice about what happens to them, and contact their support before the cutoff if anything's unclear.
  • Find your automations. If a plugin, script, Zapier step or shop integration calls remove.bg, note where it is so it doesn't fail silently on 2 December.
  • Pick your replacement and test it on your hardest photo. Hair, glass and product shots with text on the packaging show differences between tools fast.

Where to go next

It depends on how you used remove.bg:

  • You already pay for Canva: Canva's Background Remover is the official destination and is included with paid Canva plans.
  • You want something free with no account: an in-browser tool like Peelo removes backgrounds at your photo's full resolution, with no signup, credits or watermark. Your photo is processed on your device and never uploaded.
  • You process batches of product photos: look for batch support and check the price per photo. Peelo's batch mode is free for 3 photos at a time, and Batch Pro handles up to 200 per run for a one-time $19, with no subscription.
  • You used the API: you'll need another API provider. Peelo is a browser tool and doesn't offer an API.

Why we built Peelo

Peelo runs an open-source AI model (IS-Net, Apache-2.0) inside your browser, so there's no server receiving your photos and no reason to charge per image. It also automatically removes leftover background text and specks, a common problem with background removers when there's a sign or poster behind your subject. It's honest about its limits: very fine hair, glass and crowded scenes are the hardest cases for any remover, and Peelo includes an erase and restore brush for those.

Replace remove.bg with Peelo in two minutes

  1. Open Peelo and drop in a photo you'd normally send to remove.bg. The first run downloads the AI model once (about 91 MB); after that it loads from your browser cache.
  2. Check the result with the before/after slider. If anything's off, switch to Refine edges and use Erase or Restore.
  3. Pick your output: transparent PNG, or JPG on white, any colour, a blurred background or your own image. Marketplace photo makes a white, square, centred product shot.
  4. Download. The file keeps your original resolution (up to 16 megapixels).
  5. Got a folder of photos? Use the Batch tab: one set of settings, one ZIP out.
