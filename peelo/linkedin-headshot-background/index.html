Give your LinkedIn headshot a clean background

You don't need a studio for a professional profile photo. A well-lit phone photo plus a quiet, neutral background gets you most of the way. Here's how to swap out the kitchen, the office corridor or the holiday crowd.

LinkedIn photo basics

LinkedIn shows profile photos as a circle cropped from a square. The commonly recommended upload size is 400 × 400 pixels or larger, up to an 8 MB file. Bigger is fine and stays sharp on high-resolution screens. Your face should be the clear focus. In the circle crop, head and shoulders filling roughly 60% of the frame reads well, even at the tiny size shown next to comments.

Choosing a background

  • Soft neutral colour (light grey-blue, warm grey, off-white): calm and professional, and it works in light and dark mode. The preset uses a light grey-blue (#E9EDF2).
  • Blurred original: keeps a sense of place but takes away the clutter, like a portrait-mode photo. It also hides small edge mistakes around hair, which is useful because hair is the hardest part for any background remover.
  • Brand colour: fine for founders and creatives. Keep it muted so your face stays the focus.
  • Avoid pure black or busy patterns. They fight with the circular frame.

Framing that looks natural

A headshot is cut off at the bottom of the frame by your shoulders. If you centre a cut-out like that in a square, the shoulders end up floating in the middle with empty space below. Peelo's square framing spots that the subject touches the bottom edge of the photo and keeps it anchored there, with even space on the sides and above your head. Set the fill to around 75 to 85%.

Hair and edges

Be realistic about hair. Peelo uses a small on-device model, and flyaway strands against a busy background are its weakest spot. To get a natural result:

  • Shoot against a plain wall if you can. The less the background looks like your hair, the cleaner the cut.
  • Raise Edge softness slightly so the outline doesn't look pasted on.
  • Use the Blur background, which blends any leftover edge pixels.
  • Use Restore with a big, soft pass over the hairline if the top of your head looks clipped.

Lighting matters more than the background

Face a window and avoid overhead lights that throw shadows under your eyes. Hold the camera at eye level, a little more than arm's length away, or ask someone else to take it, and use the 2x lens if you have one, since it flatters faces more than wide-angle. Then swap the background. A well-lit face on a plain background beats a dim face in front of a perfect office.

How to do it with Peelo

  1. Open the tool with the headshot preset (soft grey, square, 80% fill).
  2. Add your photo. It stays on your device.
  3. Compare backgrounds: try a swatch, Custom or Blur. Blur is the most forgiving around hair.
  4. Adjust Subject fills so your head and shoulders sit comfortably. LinkedIn crops to a circle, so leave a little room above your head.
  5. Download JPG and upload it via your profile photo. Use LinkedIn's crop tool to fine-tune the circle.

Questions

What size should a LinkedIn profile photo be?

400 × 400 pixels or larger is the usual recommendation, with a maximum file size of 8 MB. Peelo keeps your photo's resolution, so a phone photo gives a much larger square, which is fine.

What background colour is best for LinkedIn?

A soft neutral such as light grey, grey-blue or warm off-white looks professional and works on light and dark themes. A gently blurred real background also works well.

My hair looks cut out. How do I fix it?

Raise Edge softness a little, use the Blur background, and brush over the hairline with Restore. Flyaway hair against a busy background is the hardest case for any remover, especially a small on-device model.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Peelo processes the photo in your browser and never uploads it.
