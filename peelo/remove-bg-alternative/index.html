What's happening with remove.bg

remove.bg's pricing page states that the standalone remove.bg service ends on 1 December 2026 and that background removal moves into Canva. That's all we're claiming here. For anything that affects your account, like remaining credits, refunds, invoices or the API, read remove.bg's own notice and FAQ and act before the cutoff. In particular, download any invoices you need while your account is still open.

If you only need to cut a subject out of a photo now and then, you don't have to pick a new subscription today. Most people fall into one of three groups:

  • Already on Canva Pro or Teams: use Canva's Background Remover. It's included with paid plans, and it's where remove.bg is going.
  • Doing product photos in volume: look at Photoroom or another tool with batch editing. Check its current pricing for full-resolution exports.
  • Doing the occasional cutout, or needing privacy: use a free in-browser tool like Peelo. Nothing is uploaded and there's no account.

Where Peelo is genuinely good, and where it isn't

Peelo runs a 4.6 MB open-source model (U²-Net small, Apache-2.0) directly in your browser. The model looks at a 320 × 320 copy of your photo, and that mask is then smoothed and applied to your original pixels. Your download keeps the full resolution of the photo you added.

It handles one clear subject well: a product on a table, a person, a pet, a car or a mug. It struggles where big server models shine, with wispy hair against a busy background, wine glasses and scenes with several overlapping subjects. For those you'll either fix the edges with the Refine brush or use a server tool. We'd rather say that up front than have you find out on a deadline.

What you get in exchange is privacy (photos never leave your device), no account, no credits and no watermark. After the first visit it also works offline. You can install it from your browser menu as an app.

Features remove.bg users ask about

  • White or coloured backgrounds: pick white, any colour, a blurred version of the original, or your own image.
  • Marketplace photos: one click gives a pure white background on a square canvas with the product centred at 85% fill.
  • Logos and signatures: a "Plain background" method keys out flat white or solid colours. For flat artwork it's often cleaner than AI.
  • PNG or JPG: PNG keeps transparency; JPG is smaller and flattens onto your chosen background.

How to switch in two minutes

  1. Save what you need from remove.bg first. Download past results and invoices before 1 December 2026, and read its notice about your credits or plan.
  2. Open Peelo and drop in a photo you'd normally have sent to remove.bg. The first run downloads the AI engine once (about 16 MB). After that it loads from your browser cache.
  3. Check the edges with the before/after slider. If something's off, switch to Refine edges and use Erase or Restore.
  4. Choose the output: transparent PNG, or JPG on white or any colour. For listings, use Marketplace photo.
  5. Download. The file keeps your photo's original pixel dimensions (up to 16 MP).
  6. Optional: install Peelo from your browser's menu ("Install app" or "Add to Home Screen") so it's one tap away and works offline.

Questions

When does remove.bg shut down?

remove.bg's pricing page says its standalone service ends on 1 December 2026 and moves into Canva. Check remove.bg's own notice for details about credits, plans and the API.

Is Peelo really free at full resolution?

Yes. Single images download free at the original resolution, up to 16 megapixels, with no account, credits or watermark. Processing happens on your device, so there's no server cost to pass on. A batch mode is planned as a one-time purchase.

Is the quality as good as remove.bg?

Not always. Peelo uses a small on-device model. It's good on single clear subjects but weaker than large server models on fine hair, glass and busy scenes. The erase and restore brush helps, but for very hard images a server-based tool may do better.

Do I need a Canva account to keep removing backgrounds?

Only if you want to use Canva's tool. Canva's help centre says its Background Remover is included with paid plans. Peelo is independent and needs no account.

Does Peelo have an API?

No. Peelo is a browser tool. If you used remove.bg's API, read remove.bg's notice for its recommended path.
