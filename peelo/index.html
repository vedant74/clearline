    Questions

    Is this a free remove.bg alternative?

    Yes. Removing a background and downloading the full-resolution PNG or JPG is free, with no account, credits, watermark or ads. Batch mode is free for 3 photos at a time; Batch Pro removes that cap for a one-time payment.

    Can I remove a background for free at full resolution?

    Yes. The cutout is applied to your original image, so the download has the same pixel dimensions as the photo you added (up to 16 megapixels; larger photos are scaled to 16 MP so they don't crash your browser).

    Can I remove a background without uploading my photo?

    Yes. The AI model is downloaded once to your browser and runs on your device. Your image is never sent to a server. After the first visit the model loads from your browser's cache.

    What happens when remove.bg shuts down?

    remove.bg's pricing page states its standalone service ends on 1 December 2026 and moves into Canva. This tool is an independent, free option that does not need a Canva or any other account.

    What if the AI gets the edges wrong?

    Switch to Refine edges. The erase and restore brush (with adjustable size) fixes leftover background or missing parts, and the edge softness slider controls how crisp the outline is.

    How does batch mode work?

    Choose or drop many photos, pick the settings once (background, marketplace square, PNG or JPG, max size), and Peelo processes them one after another in your browser. You get one ZIP with your original filenames plus a suffix. Free batches are up to 3 photos; Batch Pro is up to 200 per run for $19 once. Keep the tab open while it runs.

    Is Batch Pro a subscription? Can I get a refund?

    No subscription: you pay once and keep it in the browser you unlock it in. Payment is handled by Stripe; we never see your card. If it doesn't work for you, contact us within 14 days for a refund.

    Which images work best?

    Photos with one clear subject: products, people, pets, cars, food. Busy scenes with many subjects, glass and very fine hair are harder; use the refine brush for those.
