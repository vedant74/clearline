Remove the background from your signature

A clean, transparent signature image is handy for letters, invoices and forms you fill in yourself. Since it's your signature, it's also something you probably don't want to upload to a random website. Here's how to make one privately.

Why privacy matters for this one

Most online background removers upload your image to a server. For a product photo that hardly matters. For your signature it's worth avoiding. Peelo runs entirely in your browser: the image is decoded, processed and saved on your own device, and no copy is sent anywhere. You can check this yourself. Load the page, switch your device to airplane mode, then process the signature.

Capture a clean signature

Most of the quality comes from the photo, not the software.

  • Paper: plain white printer paper, no lines or grid.
  • Pen: a black or dark blue felt-tip or gel pen. Thin ballpoint lines are faint and break up.
  • Size: sign larger than usual, about 8 to 10 cm wide. You can shrink it later, but you can't add detail.
  • Light: bright, even daylight from a window. Avoid your own shadow and the phone's shadow falling across the page.
  • Angle: hold the phone flat and parallel to the paper, and fill the frame with the signature plus a small margin.

Using a scanner? Scan in colour or greyscale at 300 dpi or more, then crop close to the signature.

Why the Plain background method, not AI

A signature is thin lines on paper, which isn't the kind of "subject" photo AI is trained on. The Plain background method samples the paper colour from the edges of the image and makes everything close to it transparent, keeping the ink. It also handles slightly grey or cream paper, as long as the light is even. If one corner is in shadow, that area may survive as a grey smudge. Paint it out with Erase.

Getting crisp, natural edges

  • Edge softness sets how much faint ink survives. Lower it for a bolder, cleaner line. Raise it to keep light pen pressure and tapering strokes.
  • Preview on white and on a light grey to make sure no paper texture remains.
  • Download as PNG. JPG has no transparency and would bring back a background.

Use it responsibly

Only make images of your own signature, or one you're authorised to use. A pasted image is convenient for documents you send yourself, but it isn't the same as a verified e-signature. For contracts, use the signing process the other party asks for.

How to do it with Peelo

  1. Photograph or scan your signature as described above: dark pen, white paper, even light.
  2. Open the tool with the signature preset. No AI download is needed for this method.
  3. Add the photo. The paper disappears and the ink stays.
  4. Tidy up: adjust Edge softness, and use Refine edges, then Erase, on any shadows or specks.
  5. Download the PNG and insert it into your document. In Word or Google Docs, set the image to "In front of text" and scale it down.

Questions

Is it safe to make a signature image online?

With Peelo, the image never leaves your device. Processing runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. You can confirm this by turning on airplane mode after the page loads.

Why is there a grey patch around my signature?

That's usually a shadow or uneven light on the paper. Retake the photo in brighter, even light, or remove the patch with Refine edges and then Erase.

Why are parts of my signature faint or broken?

Thin or light pen strokes are close to the paper colour. Lower Edge softness slightly, or sign again with a darker felt-tip pen.

Is a pasted signature image legally binding?

That depends on where you are and on the document. A signature image isn't a verified electronic signature. For contracts, use the signing method the other party requires.
