Make an image background transparent

A transparent background means everything around your subject is see-through, so you can put it on any colour, slide or design. Here's how to do it for free in your browser, plus which format to save in so the transparency doesn't vanish.

Which method to use

Peelo has two ways to remove a background. Picking the right one is the biggest quality win:

  • AI subject (default): for photos of people, products, pets, cars and food. The AI finds the main subject and removes everything else, including leftover background text and specks.
  • Plain background: for logos, icons, signatures, scans and screenshots on a flat white or solid colour. It removes that colour directly, which keeps sharp edges on flat artwork. See our guide to removing a white background.

Save as PNG, not JPG

Only some formats can store transparency. PNG keeps it and is what Peelo gives you for transparent cutouts. JPG can't store transparency: if you save a cutout as JPG, the see-through area turns into a solid colour (Peelo lets you pick which). WebP and GIF also support transparency, but PNG works almost everywhere: Google Slides, PowerPoint, Canva, Figma, Word and most websites.

Why does my transparent image show a white or black background?

  • The viewer is drawing a background behind it. Many image viewers and chat apps show transparency as white or black. Open it in a slide or design tool to check.
  • It was converted to JPG somewhere along the way, for example by a messaging app that compresses images. Send it as a file or document instead.
  • The checkerboard is part of the image. Some sites let you download a "transparent" image that actually has a grey-and-white checkerboard baked in. Peelo's PNGs contain real transparency.

Tips for clean edges

  • Use Edge softness to go from crisp (products, logos) to soft (hair, fur).
  • Switch to Refine edges and brush with Erase or Restore to fix any spot the AI got wrong.
  • Start from the largest version of the image you have. Peelo keeps your original resolution, up to 16 megapixels.

How to make a background transparent

  1. Open Peelo and drop in your image, choose it with the file picker, or paste it.
  2. Pick the method: AI subject for photos, Plain background for logos and flat graphics.
  3. Keep the background on Transparent (the default) and check the edges with the before/after slider.
  4. Fix any spots in Refine edges with Erase or Restore.
  5. Download PNG. The transparency is preserved at full resolution.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Transparent PNG downloads are free at full resolution, with no account, credits or watermark.

Does my image get uploaded?

No. The AI model runs in your browser, so the image never leaves your device.

Why is my transparent PNG showing a white background?

Usually the app you're viewing it in draws white behind transparent areas, or the image was converted to JPG when it was sent. Open it in a design or slide tool to check.

Can I make a transparent background on my phone?

Yes. Peelo works in mobile browsers. Very large photos may be scaled to 16 megapixels so the browser doesn't run out of memory.

Which is better for logos: AI or Plain background?

Plain background, if the logo sits on a flat white or solid colour. It keeps sharp edges and small details that subject-detection AI can smooth over.
