Remove backgrounds from Shopify product photos and keep them consistent

On Shopify, your theme puts products side by side in collection grids, so mismatched sizes and backgrounds show up straight away. The fix is boring but effective: the same aspect ratio, the same background and the same padding on every product.

The size Shopify recommends

Shopify recommends 2048 × 2048 px for square product images. That's large enough for zoom on high-density screens without huge files. Product images can be up to 4472 × 4472 px (20 megapixels) and 20 MB. Square is the safest choice because most themes crop collection thumbnails to a fixed ratio. If yours uses portrait or landscape, keep every product at that same ratio instead.

Why consistency matters more than perfection

A collection page with one product on pure white, one on grey card and one in a kitchen looks like a marketplace, not a brand. Shoppers read that as less trustworthy. With the same background and margin on every product, mismatched photography looks deliberate. Peelo's square framing helps here because it measures the cut-out product and sizes the square around it. A tall vase and a short candle end up with the same visual weight at the same fill.

  • Decide one fill for the whole catalogue. 75 to 85% suits most stores, and 80% leaves room for sale badges that some themes overlay in a corner.
  • Decide one background: white, an off-white, or a brand tint. Write down the hex code and use Custom every time.
  • Decide one file type. JPG is smaller and loads faster. Use PNG only if your theme's design needs transparent product images, for example on a coloured section background.

Transparent PNG or JPG on a colour?

A transparent PNG lets the theme's background show through, which looks great on a tinted section. It's also larger, and if you later change the theme colour, your images change with it. A JPG on your chosen colour is predictable and lighter. Shopify serves images through its CDN in optimised sizes, but starting with a sensible file still helps your page weight. Most stores do best with JPG on white or off-white.

Variants and colour accuracy

When each colour variant gets its own photo, removing the background makes the variants easy to compare. Remember the cut-out doesn't change the product's pixels, so colour accuracy still comes from your lighting and camera. Shoot every variant in the same setup. If a variant looks off, fix it in your photo editor before removing the background.

How to do it with Peelo

  1. Open the tool with the Shopify starting preset, or use Marketplace photo in the tool.
  2. Add a product photo. Shoot so the product is large in the frame. Peelo keeps your original pixels and never upscales, so a 2048 px square needs the product to span about 1650 px at 80% fill.
  3. Set your store background (white or your brand hex) and keep it the same for every product.
  4. Refine any missing straps, spouts or labels with Restore, and remove stray bits with Erase.
  5. Download JPG (or PNG for transparent) and upload it in Shopify admin under Products → Media.

Questions

What image size does Shopify recommend?

Shopify recommends 2048 × 2048 px for square product images. Images can be up to 4472 × 4472 px (20 megapixels) and 20 MB.

Should Shopify product images be PNG or JPG?

JPG for most stores: it's smaller and faster. Use a transparent PNG only when your theme shows products on a coloured or patterned section and you want that background to show through.

How do I make all my product photos the same size?

Use the square framing with the same Subject fills percentage for every product. Peelo measures the cut-out and builds the square around it, so items of different shapes get consistent margins.

Is there a bulk option for a whole catalogue?

Not yet. Peelo processes one image at a time. A one-time-purchase batch mode with saved presets is planned.
