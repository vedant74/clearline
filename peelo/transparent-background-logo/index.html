Make a logo's background transparent

You have a logo as a JPG on white, and you need a transparent PNG for a slide, a website header or a product mock-up. AI subject detection is the wrong tool for flat artwork. Peelo has a Plain background method that removes the white itself.

Why logos need a different method

Background-removal AI is trained to find "the subject" of a photo, like a person or a product. A logo isn't a photo. It might be three separate letters, a thin outline or a shape with holes in it, and an AI model may keep the main shape and drop the rest. The Plain background method works differently. It samples the colour around the edges of your image (usually white), then makes every pixel close to that colour transparent, with soft edges for anti-aliased lines. Letters, outlines and holes are all handled the same way.

Start from the best source you have

  • If you have a vector file (SVG, AI, EPS or a PDF from the designer), export a transparent PNG from it. That will always beat removing a background from a JPG.
  • If you only have a raster file, use the largest version you can find. A 2000 px logo gives far cleaner edges than a 200 px favicon grabbed from a website.
  • Avoid screenshots of logos. They add compression blur and a coloured fringe.

Avoiding white halos

A logo on white has edge pixels that are a blend of logo colour and white. Remove the white and put the logo on a dark background, and you can see a faint light outline, or "halo". Use the Edge softness slider to control it:

  • Lower softness makes the cut tighter and removes more of the near-white fringe. This is best if the logo will sit on dark backgrounds.
  • Higher softness gives smoother edges. This is best on light backgrounds.

Before you download, preview with the Colour background set to black to see any halo, then switch back to Transparent.

Watch out for white inside the logo

The Plain background method removes every pixel that matches the background colour, including white inside the logo, like white text on a red badge. If parts of your logo should stay white:

  • use Refine edges and then Restore to paint those areas back, or
  • switch the method to AI subject, which keeps a solid badge shape together.

Logos on a coloured background

It doesn't have to be white. If your logo sits on flat blue, the method samples blue from the edges and removes that instead. It works best when the background is one flat colour without gradients or texture.

How to do it with Peelo

  1. Open the tool with the logo preset. It sets the Plain background method and transparent output. The AI model isn't needed, so it starts instantly.
  2. Add your logo image (JPG, PNG or WebP). Use the largest version you have.
  3. Check for halos. Switch the background to Colour and pick black, then adjust Edge softness until the edge looks clean.
  4. Restore any white parts that belong to the logo with Refine edges and then Restore.
  5. Set the background back to Transparent and download the PNG. JPG can't store transparency.

Questions

Why does my transparent logo have a white outline?

The edge pixels in the original are a mix of logo colour and white. Lower the Edge softness slider for a tighter cut, and preview on black before downloading.

Part of my logo disappeared. Why?

The Plain background method removes every pixel that matches the background colour, including white areas inside the logo. Paint them back with Restore, or switch to the AI subject method.

Can I get an SVG?

No. Peelo outputs PNG and JPG. If you need a vector logo, ask your designer for the source file, or use a vector tracing tool on the transparent PNG.

Is my logo uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens in your browser. That matters for unreleased brand work under NDA.
