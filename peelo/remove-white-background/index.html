Remove a white background from an image

A white background is the most common one people want gone: logos saved as JPG, scanned signatures, screenshots, product shots on white. Peelo has a method built for exactly this, so you get crisp, transparent edges without the white halo.

Two cases, two methods

  • Flat white behind flat artwork (a logo, icon, signature, scan or diagram): use Plain background. It removes the white directly, pixel by pixel, which keeps thin lines and small text intact. The link above opens Peelo with this already selected.
  • A photo on a white backdrop (a product shot, a person against a white wall): use AI subject. Real photos have shadows and gradients that aren't pure white, and the AI separates the subject from them better.

Getting rid of the white halo

The thin white outline you sometimes see around a cutout comes from edge pixels that are part subject, part white. To clean it up:

  • In Plain background mode, raise Edge softness slightly so edge pixels fade out instead of keeping a white fringe.
  • If your design has a dark background, test the PNG on dark. Halos are invisible on white and obvious on black.
  • Start from the highest-resolution original you have; JPG compression adds speckles around edges.

When white is part of your subject

If the logo or product itself has white areas (white text inside a dark badge, a white mug), Plain background can make those see-through too. Two fixes:

  • Switch to AI subject, which keeps the whole object as long as it's clearly separate from the background.
  • Or stay in Plain background and use Refine edges → Restore to paint the white parts back in.

How to remove a white background

  1. Open Peelo with Plain background, or open Peelo and choose Plain background under Cut-out method.
  2. Drop in your image. Peelo detects the background colour from the edges, usually white.
  3. Adjust Edge softness until the edges look clean, with no white fringe.
  4. Restore anything that should stay white using Refine edges → Restore.
  5. Download PNG to keep the transparency.

Questions

What's the best way to remove a white background from a logo?

Use the Plain background method. It removes flat white directly and keeps sharp edges and small text, which subject-detection AI can soften.

Why does my logo have a white outline after removing the background?

Edge pixels are a mix of logo colour and white. Raise Edge softness a little and check the result on a dark background.

Can it remove off-white or grey backgrounds?

Yes. Plain background removes whatever solid colour is around the edges, not just pure white. For photos with shadows or gradients, use AI subject instead.

Is it free and private?

Yes. It's free at full resolution with no account, and the image is processed in your browser without being uploaded.
