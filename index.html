Why these tools exist

A lot of everyday tools have turned into traps: QR code generators whose "free" codes stop working after a trial, background removers that charge credits for a full-size download or upload your photos to a server. Hyphenatesi builds the simple, honest version.

Both tools run in your browser. Peelo's AI model runs on your own device, so your photos are never uploaded. ForeverQR makes static QR codes that point straight to your link, so there's nothing to expire. Free versions stay free, and anything paid is a fair, one-time price.

Our promise: the free versions stay free, and anything paid is priced fairly with a clear way to cancel.
