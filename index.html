Tax IDs, handled carefully

  • Vendors enter their details on a secure form, never by replying to an email.
  • A one-time code confirms the vendor before they can open the form.
  • The W-9 is signed online, with the IRS certification shown right above the signature.
  • After your approval, the details and the signed PDF go into the client's QuickBooks.
  • Clearline then deletes its copy and keeps only the status and date.

FAQ

Common questions

Does Clearline file the 1099s?

No. Clearline makes sure every vendor is tagged and has a W-9 on file. You file the same way you do today, through QuickBooks or your filing service.

Which accounting software does it work with?

QuickBooks Online first. Xero is planned for later.

Will it change anything in my clients' books on its own?

No. Scans are read-only. Nothing is written to QuickBooks until you approve a submitted W-9.

How does it decide who needs a 1099?

It looks at 2026 payments per vendor, how they were paid, and the vendor's entity type. Card and PayPal payments are reported by the processor, most corporations are exempt, and attorneys are flagged even when incorporated. You make the final call.
